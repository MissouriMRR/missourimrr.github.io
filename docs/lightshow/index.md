# Lightshow

Hello! Here is where the documentation for the Lightshow subteam of
the Multirotor Design Team is located.

## What is the Lightshow Team

We are one of several of the subteams that comprise the S&T Multirotor Design Team.
Our goal is to create software that enables S&T to host various drone lightshow events.

We ourselves are divided into subteams. 
One subteam handles the ground station and the other handles the drone code.
